How to read this report

Story (narrative). Posts that say roughly the same thing are clustered into one story. Bubble size is the number of posts; a red bubble means most of its posts come from accounts flagged as coordinated.

Coordinated group. Accounts linked by independent signals: near-identical text, posting within minutes of each other, reshare loops, reused avatars, accounts created together, or the same handle across platforms. More signals together means higher confidence.

Origin. The earliest post of each story per platform, how long each platform lagged, and whether the first wave was coordinated.

Limitations. Coordination is not proof of fake accounts: real fans, journalists and friends also repeat each other. Only public posts are read, a platform can return a partial sample, and every group is a lead for a human analyst to review, not attribution.
