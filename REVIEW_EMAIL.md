Subject: coordwatch: Coordinated Activity & Misinformation Detection (take-home submission)

Hello,

Please find my submission for the "Coordinated Activity and Misinformation Detection" task.

Repository: https://github.com/danishfaraz7/coordwatch

What it does: you enter a topic, dates and 2-3 platforms (YouTube, Telegram, X). It collects public posts, groups them into stories, finds accounts that appear to act together (with the evidence), traces where each story started and how it moved between platforms, and exports JSON/CSV, a web report and an executive PDF. It runs at zero cost.

Fastest way to see it (about 5 minutes, no keys, nothing to install except a browser):
1. Open GETTING_STARTED.md in the repository, "Path A: GitHub Codespaces".
2. Follow the 10 steps, then click "Try Demo".
3. Use "Take the tour" in the same file to walk through every screen.
A pre-generated sample output is in out/example/ (open report.html).

Documents: GETTING_STARTED.md (single setup + navigation guide, written for non-technical readers); README.md (full features, methodology, limits, how AI was used and checked).

Honest notes: live collectors were tested on recorded-shape fixtures, not at scale; free X gives a sample without engagement numbers; Telegram free-text search needs a Premium account. Details in README section 15.
